## Why

Alshami is an Australian business importing two authentic Syrian brands — Alshami Coffee and Al-Attar Herbs — and currently has no web presence. The website will serve as the primary informational touchpoint for Australian customers, distributors, and stockists to discover the brands and their product ranges.

## What Changes

- New Next.js website built from scratch with 5 pages: Home, About Us, Alshami Coffee, Al-Attar Herbs, and Contact
- Full Alshami design system implementation (warm cream canvas, four-tier brown palette, pill buttons, dark espresso feature bands)
- Framer Motion animations throughout (page transitions, scroll reveals, hero text reveals, card animations)
- Fully responsive layout across mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Video hero banner on the Home page using the provided `media/videos/banner.mp4`
- Product showcases for all 6 Alshami Coffee products and 4 Al-Attar Herbs products
- Contact page with phone, email, WhatsApp, and social media links
- SEO metadata on every page

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `navigation`: Global nav with logo, page links, and responsive hamburger menu
- `home-page`: Hero with video background, brand introductions, and CTAs to brand pages
- `about-page`: Business story, mission, and the Syrian heritage narrative
- `alshami-coffee-page`: Brand history, key facts, and full product grid (6 products)
- `al-attar-herbs-page`: Brand history, key facts, and full product grid (4 products)
- `contact-page`: Contact details including phone, email, WhatsApp, and social links
- `footer`: Shared footer with navigation links, brand info, and dark espresso background

## Impact

- New Next.js project scaffolded from scratch
- Dependencies: Next.js (latest), Tailwind CSS (latest), shadcn/ui (latest), Framer Motion
- All images served from `media/images/`, video from `media/videos/banner.mp4`
- Global CSS defines all design system colour tokens
- No backend, database, or API integrations — purely static/informational
