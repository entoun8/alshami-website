# Alshami — Product Information

## Business

Alshami is an Australian business that imports and distributes two authentic Syrian brands — Alshami Coffee and Al-Attar Herbs — directly from Damascus, Syria, to customers across Australia.

---

## Alshami Coffee

Alshami Coffee is a historic Syrian coffee brand established in Damascus in 1924. It is known for producing authentic, premium-quality Turkish and Arabic coffee blends that reflect traditional Levantine coffee culture.

### Key Facts
- Founded in Damascus, Syria, in 1924
- Over 100 years of heritage
- Specialises in Turkish coffee and Arabic coffee
- Uses premium Arabica coffee beans finely ground and blended with natural green cardamom
- Uses a combination of traditional and modern roasting techniques
- Exported worldwide and available in Middle Eastern specialty grocers globally

### Products

**Extra Cardamom Blend (Blue Packaging)**
A strong, highly aromatic blend with a pungent cardamom flavour. One of their most popular products.

**Extra Cardamom Blend (Gold Packaging)**
A premium aromatic cardamom blend with a slightly refined profile.

**Plain Turkish Coffee**
Classic Turkish coffee with no added spices, for those who prefer pure coffee.

**Mocha Blend**
A green Mocha coffee blend — smooth and earthy in flavour.

**Instant Sachets**
Convenient, ready-to-brew sachets for quick preparation of traditional coffee.

**Arabic Coffee Mix**
A ready-to-mix traditional Arabic coffee blend.

## Al-Attar Herbs and Teas

Al-Attar for Biopharmaceutical Industries is a Syrian brand established in Damascus in 1990. It is one of the pioneering herbal pharmaceutical companies in the Arab world, combining traditional Syrian herbal remedies with modern scientific research.

### Key Facts
- Founded in Damascus, Syria, in 1990
- Over 35 years of expertise
- Products are developed in specialised research laboratories
- Formulated in accordance with international pharmacological references for natural medicine
- 100% natural — no artificial colours, preservatives, or chemical stimulants
- Allergen-free
- Products come in individually wrapped tea bags
- Exported worldwide and available in supermarkets, pharmacies, and Middle Eastern specialty grocers globally

### Products

**Zhourat Shamia**
A classic and traditional Syrian blend of aromatic herbs and flowers. Includes chamomile, lemon verbena, Damask rose, and green tea. Calming and caffeine-free.

**Chamomile**
Pure chamomile, known for its calming and soothing properties.

**Aniseed and Fennel**
A digestive herbal blend, warming and aromatic.

**Cumin and Lemon**
A digestive support herb blend with a citrus twist.
